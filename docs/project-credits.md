# Name and credits

**MATRIX** stands for **Matrix Architecture for Terraforming Realistic Environments in X**. The application and repository are currently named MATREX VR. X spans land, water and air. The name nods to science-fiction worlds and recursive names in open-source software.

Designed and conceptualized by **Pavan Kumar Kaushik**, with **August Paula** and **Chi-Yu Lee**.

[Source repository](https://github.com/pvnkmrksk/matrexVR) · [Software citation](../CITATION.cff)

The application keeps these credits in its About dialog, opened from the MATREX brand. They do not occupy the experiment or recording overlays.

Third-party libraries, fonts, icons and assets retain their own licenses. Copies of the supplied library/font/icon notices are bundled under `StreamingAssets/ThirdPartyNotices` and available in **About → Third-party notices**. Unity and vendor assets retain their original package notices and license terms. This checkout does not contain a repository-wide LICENSE file; these acknowledgements do not assign a new license to the project or its third-party assets.
