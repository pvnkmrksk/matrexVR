## Hardware recovery

The [catalogue](../site/hardware/index.html) and [illustrated worksheet](assembly.md) cover **five named source groups, nine unique mesh shapes**, with 18 individual STL/3MF exports. An inventory records **154 filename-matched local candidates**. Original projects remain in local Downloads. Unity Blender animal/cylinder visual assets were excluded from printable-kit claims; no additional relevant CAD/print matches were found in the Documents/Desktop filename scan.

The source groups are a Locust MATREX camera holder, middle LED-panel holder, top-panel mount filename variant P1.952/128×128, ball-holder filename variant 26.5, and table-clamp filename variant 7. These were selected as representative recoverable candidates; neither their revision ordering nor their compatibility has been inferred from filenames.

Each selected source declares millimetres. Nested 3MF component references and transforms are resolved; mirrored transforms preserve triangle winding. Source rotation is retained, plate translation removed, and repeated identical shapes collapsed while plate counts remain recorded. Exported STLs have verified binary lengths and finite coordinates; geometry-only 3MF archives reopen. Bounds agree to 0.0001 mm between the recorded measurements and exports. All nine recovered meshes have zero indexed boundary/non-manifold edges and zero degenerate triangles. These checks do not establish self-intersection freedom, mechanical fit, tolerances or printability.

The [4.0 MiB review bundle](../site/hardware/matrex-hardware-review.zip) includes the geometry, actual mesh illustrations, inventory and offline HTML/CSS documentation. Original printer profiles, personal designer identifiers and machine-specific settings are excluded. There is no G-code. 3MF is editable **mesh geometry**, not parametric CAD history. An unmatched Flexure System STEP file is inventoried but not misrepresented as a verified matching source.

The worksheet provides part identification, measured outer dimensions in the catalogue, a candidate BOM, unit/import checks, source-orientation evidence, printing-review guidance, a staged dry-fit checklist and links to software/display/tracking setup. It explicitly marks unknown quantities and interfaces.
