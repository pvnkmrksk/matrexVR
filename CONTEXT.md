# MATREX VR experiments

MATREX VR presents visual stimuli to tracked animals in closed-loop experiments. Kannadi can present generated swarms and representations of tracked locusts.

## Kannadi populations

**Simulated locust**

A member of the generated swarm whose motion comes from the simulation.

**Peer avatar**

The primary virtual body that represents another tracked locust to the observing locust.

**Avatar copy**

An additional virtual body that follows the same tracked locust as its peer avatar, at a spatial offset.

**Shared arena**

The fixed virtual area occupied by the tracked locusts and the single generated swarm. The same simulated individual occupies the same position for every observer.

**Per-rig body count**

The total number of simulated locusts, peer avatars, and avatar copies presented to a rig in the shared arena, counted before camera clipping and occlusion.
